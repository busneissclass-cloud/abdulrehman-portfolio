Case study

Thekedar.

DIRECTORY · LIVE

01

The challenge.

Finding a trustworthy builder or contractor in Lahore is a word-of-mouth lottery. Ask around, get a number, hope for the best. Meanwhile, good contractors — the ones who show up, finish on time and do honest work — have almost no way to be found except through referrals. They are invisible to the homeowner two neighbourhoods over who needs exactly their skill. Thekedar set out to fix both sides of that problem with a free directory of verified builders and contractors in Lahore, with zero commission and WhatsApp-first contact.

The challenge was structural. A directory is a two-sided marketplace: it needs contractors to list and homeowners to search, and each side is useless without the other. Getting contractors to list for free required removing every barrier — no listing fees, no platform commission, no complicated onboarding. Getting homeowners to trust the directory required making verification real and visible, because a "verified" badge that means nothing is worse than no badge at all; it trains users to ignore trust signals.

And then there was the contact problem. Directories love to insert themselves between the customer and the professional — gated messages, platform fees, response delays. Thekedar's promise is the opposite: customers contact professionals directly on WhatsApp. The build had to make that direct connection instant and frictionless while keeping the directory itself useful as a discovery surface.

02

Strategy.

The strategy was to be the anti-platform: free listings, zero commission, direct contact. Every marketplace instinct says to take a cut and control the conversation; Thekedar's positioning says the opposite, and that opposition is the whole brand. The site was built to make that positioning unmistakable — "free", "verified", "zero commission" and "WhatsApp-first" are not buried in an about page, they are the headline.

Supply comes first. The directory only works if contractors list, so the listing experience was designed to be free, fast and obviously worthwhile. Verified professionals list for free, and the verification badge does the selling for them — it is a visible, public signal that this contractor has been checked. The badge system was conceived as the directory's currency: the more it means, the more contractors want it, and the more customers trust it.

Demand was planned around local search. Homeowners search for contractors the way they search for everything local: "builder in DHA Lahore", "plumber near me", service plus area. Local SEO was therefore a foundation decision, not a marketing afterthought — category and profile pages structured to capture service-plus-area queries, with the directory architecture organised the way Lahoris actually search.

03

Execution.

Directory architecture. Contractor profiles organised by trade and area, so a homeowner can find the right professional the way they would ask a neighbour: "who does tiles in Johar Town?" Clean URL structure per trade and location, with profile pages that carry the details that matter — services, areas served, verification status, and a way to reach them now.

Verification badges. A visible verification system on profiles. The badge is designed to be earned and displayed prominently, so customers reach out with confidence from the first message instead of spending the first conversation testing whether the contractor is legitimate.

WhatsApp-first contact. Every profile routes to direct WhatsApp contact — no platform messaging layer, no fees, no waiting for a portal to forward a lead. The customer and the contractor talk directly, which is exactly what both sides prefer in Pakistan.

Local SEO foundations. Trade-plus-area page architecture, metadata and heading discipline, local business schema, and mobile-first performance — because directory traffic is search traffic, and search traffic in this category is overwhelmingly mobile and local.

Tracked and owned. Analytics and Search Console from launch, and the business owns the domain, directory, data and logins with full documentation. Nothing held hostage.

05

Outcome.

The objective was a working two-sided directory: contractors list for free because the verification badge is worth having, homeowners search because the listings are trustworthy, and neither side pays a platform fee. The build was designed to serve that objective directly.

The trade-plus-area architecture exists so homeowners find professionals the way they search. The verification badges exist so the first message starts with confidence. The WhatsApp-first contact exists so discovery turns into a conversation instantly, without a platform standing in the middle. And the zero-commission positioning exists so the directory has a reason to be chosen over word of mouth. I will not invent listing counts or enquiry volumes — but the directory ships with the mechanics a local marketplace needs: free supply, searchable demand, visible trust, and measurement from day one.

See it live: thekedar.com.pk
