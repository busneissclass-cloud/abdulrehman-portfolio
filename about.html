My story

Most marketers rent attention. I shape the entire system that attention flows into: the landing pages, the tracking, the follow-up automation that turns a click into a customer. When search, advertising and development are planned together, the customer journey gets clearer — and the work gets easier to measure.

Principles

How I work. Every time.

Four habits guide every engagement — from a one-week audit to a year-long retainer. They're how the work stays honest, measurable and built to last.

🔍

Audit first

I study your website, ad accounts, analytics and competitors before recommending anything. The plan is ranked by revenue impact — not by what's easiest to sell.

🛠️

Build in the open

Pages, campaigns and automations go live with you watching. Structured review rounds, clear milestones, no surprises — you always know what's happening and why.

📊

Measure everything

Tracking is installed before a single rupee is spent. Every click, call and sale traces back to the decision that caused it — so growth is provable, not promised.

🔥

Optimize relentlessly

We scale what wins and cut what doesn't. Compounding growth comes from iteration — testing, learning and improving month after month — not from one big launch.
